The bundled board fonts are from Google Fonts, revision
6ebe737e44fd64ee736bbc6feed6136a6dbfd222.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/6ebe737e44fd64ee736bbc6feed6136a6dbfd222

Kalam, Patrick Hand, Redacted Script, Indie Flower, Gochi Hand, Montserrat,
Manrope, Roboto and JetBrains Mono are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. Luckiest Guy and Schoolbell are distributed under the Apache
License 2.0. The unmodified upstream copyright and license files are included
for each family.

ExcaliDash generated static 400 and 700 instances of the upstream variable
TrueType files for Montserrat, Manrope, Roboto and JetBrains Mono, keeping
any other axes at their defaults. The TrueType files are used for server
rendering and repackaged as WOFF2 for web delivery.
